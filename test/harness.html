The lighthouse keeper

Every evening he climbed the spiral staircase to light the lamp.

The sea was restless that winter and few ships passed the point.

He kept a journal of the weather and the birds he saw.

  • Salt, flour and lamp oil arrived once a month.
  • The supply boat never came in a storm.
